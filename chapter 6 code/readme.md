## When journalists share their happy feelings

This folder contains the scripts used in chapter 6 'When journalists share their happy feelings. Affective connection as a source of reliability in personal journalism'. This chapter is currently also under review to be published as an article.

1. dictionary_emo_words contains the dictionary that I have constructed of emotion nouns, verbs and adjectives in ethos utterances based on appraisal theory
2. posnegappraisal analyses:
        - the frequency of emotions over time in the entire dataset and in the personal journalism sample
        - the frequency of positive versus negative emotions over time in the entire dataset
